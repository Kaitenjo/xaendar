Error encyclopedia

The messages of the compiler, the build, the runtime, the signals and the CLI, as they are printed, each with its cause and its fix. Paste a part of a message in the filter to find it.

Template errors start with Xaendar: Failed to compile template and the path of the template, followed by the phase in brackets, the line and the column, and the excerpt involved. Errors of the type checker without a phase are the messages of TypeScript, checked against the class of the component. Positions and paths are left out below.
